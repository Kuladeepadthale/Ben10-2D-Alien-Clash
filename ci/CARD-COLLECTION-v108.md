# v10.8: collect cards, then unlock

Matching pack draws accumulate separately for each alien. Common (previously named Normal) requires 5 copies; Rare 10; Epic 15; Heroic 20; Legendary 25. Packs do not add aliens to ownership or grant unlock XP. Once the counter is complete, open the alien details and press Unlock to claim it without diamonds. The collection shows card progress or Unlock Ready. Each reveal records the progress at that draw; revealing and reloading never re-award cards.

Existing unlocked aliens, free starters, upgrades and currency are retained. Direct diamond unlocks remain available. Extra cards for an already unlocked alien, or one whose required card count is complete, give the existing one-diamond duplicate reward. A failed save restores coins, diamonds, cards, ownership and the pack receipt together. Partial progress uses the existing local/cloud profile payload.

Multi-hit melee clips play a fresh impact, follow-through and retraction for each existing damage tick. Two-pose clips reuse the alien's native normal-attack recovery pose, keeping the whole body connected. Small whole-body thrust and recoil follow each strike. Villains also reset between successive strikes. Tail-specific actions retain their dedicated animation. Damage, reach, cooldowns and total hit count are unchanged.

Validation: all five thresholds, explicit claim, one-time XP, repeated draws within one pack, overflow duplicates, partial reveal reload, save failure rollback and old-profile migration; all alien multi-hit melee moves in both directions, every beat's strike/recoil/reset and all villain multi-hit combinations; phone layout checks include progress and the claim button.
