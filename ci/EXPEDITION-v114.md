# Omnitrix Expedition 11.4

New main mode: six encounters across Bellwood, the Badlands and the Null Void. One alien per run. Health and ultimate carry between fights. At camp, choose a medkit (+25% maximum health recovery), power surge (+15% damage) or shield core (+15% maximum health). Bonuses are run-only. Safe routes give40 loot; risky routes give65 loot and20 ultimate against a stronger enemy. Completing the journey pays500 coins plus earned loot and Hero XP; a completed defeat keeps loot plus20 coins. Quitting gives no reward. Reloading ends an active run. Best journey, six new reward missions, and completed-run stats persist.

Boss Rush now centers five large named portraits with fight order. Landscape fits all five; portrait has a swipeable full-body lineup. Rules remain available in a dialog. Existing rules, lineup and rewards are preserved.
