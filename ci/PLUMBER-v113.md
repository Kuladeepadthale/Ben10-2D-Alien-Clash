# Plumber Missions 11.3

Adds Null Void Lockdown before Boss Rush in a horizontal operation-card lane. Swipe, snap, arrows and keyboard arrow keys work; cards stay readable in portrait and landscape.

## Null Void Lockdown
Hold the center ring while grounded to build 25 seconds of seal progress before a 90-second combat timer expires. Taking damage interrupts sealing for 1.25 seconds. A defeated villain gives 3 seconds of seal progress and restores 10% maximum health. New villains return without resetting player health or ultimate charge. A sealed portal pays 300 coins plus Hero XP; a completed defeat pays 20 coins; quitting pays nothing. Reward payout occurs once. Saved best seal and six new reward goals use existing local/cloud save paths. Classic/Galactic progress and Boss Rush rules remain intact.
